Education Profile of Jyot Kalpesh Patel

Jyot Kalpesh Patel completed a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) from Natubhai V Patel College of Pure and Applied Sciences (NVPAS), under CVM University (CVMU), located in Vallabh Vidyanagar, Gujarat, India.

He studied there from 2020 to 2023 and graduated with a CGPA of 9.88. He secured 2nd Rank at the university level.

During his BCA, Jyot served as General Secretary (GS), was an active member of the Creative Expression (CE) Portfolio, and participated as an NSS Volunteer. His undergraduate journey emphasized academic excellence, leadership, student engagement, and community participation.

One memorable milestone from this period was celebrating his achievement of graduating second in the university through a LinkedIn video reel.

After completing BCA, Jyot pursued a Master of Computer Applications (MCA) at Parul Institute of Engineering and Technology (PIET), which is affiliated with Parul University (PU), located in Limda, Waghodia, Gujarat, India.

He studied from August 2023 to May 2025 and specialized in Full Stack Web Development. He graduated with a CGPA of 8.93.

During his MCA, Jyot served as the Class Representative (CR) and was actively involved in academic and leadership responsibilities.

As part of his graduation requirements, he published a Scopus-indexed review paper.
