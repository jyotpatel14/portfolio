Project Overview

The HRMS Timesheet Module was one of the first major enterprise software projects developed by Jyot Kalpesh Patel during his internship at OpenEyes Technologies Inc. The project also served as his MCA final project, allowing him to bridge academic learning with real-world software engineering practices.

The project focused on digitizing employee work logging, attendance tracking, and workforce management processes through a mobile application. The objective was to reduce manual HR coordination efforts, improve operational efficiency, and provide employees with a streamlined platform for recording work activities and attendance information.

The application was designed as an enterprise mobile solution capable of integrating with backend HR systems through secure REST APIs and real-time synchronization workflows.

Before implementation, many HR-related workflows required manual coordination and tracking.
